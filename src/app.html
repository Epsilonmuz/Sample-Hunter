YOUR HUNTS

HOW IT WORKS

1 · Hunt. Record a few seconds of anything with hits in it: knocks, taps, claps, keys, a beatbox. Or pull the sound out of any video on your phone.
2 · Kit. It chops every hit, hears which one is low, bright, noisy or musical, and designs a Kick, Snare, Hat, Perc, an 808, playable Keys and an Atmos pad from them.
3 · Beat. Pick a pocket (Trap, Drill, Dembow, Boom Bap, Afro, Jersey), hit Generate, tweak, play the pads.
4 · Take it to FL. One ZIP: the beat as a WAV, every one-shot, and the pattern as MIDI.
